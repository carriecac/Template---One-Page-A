    About Us

    Family-run. Farmer-focused.

    Prairie Gold Grain Co. has been serving local farm families since 1952. What started as a single wooden elevator beside the rail line is now a modern facility with fast receiving, accurate scales and plenty of storage.

    We know a good bid only matters if the line moves and the ticket is right. Our team lives in this community, and we treat every load like it’s our own.
